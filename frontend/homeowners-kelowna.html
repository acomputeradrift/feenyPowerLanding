What a Kelowna project needs settled before anyone is on site

Fixtures chosen controls still open

Kelowna builds and renovations often settle lights and finishes while the control system is still unnamed. We write the design and specification for Lutron, RTI, or Vantage so the company on site is not guessing what the keypads should do.

How you use the house

Tell us which rooms matter, whether the house is lived in all year, and what should happen when nobody is there. Those are design questions. We will not infer them from the fact that the property is in Kelowna.
