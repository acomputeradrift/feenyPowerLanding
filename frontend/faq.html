What is the process from first call to final sign off at commissioning?

The initial call is to determine scope, timeline and make sure that we are a good fit for success. I will ask a series of questions to determine the current criteria (rooms, sources, equipment, client wants) and to determine the level of UI that is best for your project. Some systems are what I call Overview only (the ability to control all rooms from a single page), some systems are Room-based only (navigate to a room, pick your activity) and some systems are both for the most flexibility for multiple user types. For larger systems, we can also create a floor plan-based system using the architectural drawing from the build.

From that I will use my proposal tool to produce a time budget that reflects our current understanding of the scope, usually within 5 minutes of getting all the answers.

If given the go-ahead, initial programming will begin immediately with the goal of being ready for testing on the date that the equipment arrives. That way, I am good to go well ahead of whenever your team is onsite for commissioning.

On commissioning and testing days, using either a site computer or an on-site tech’s computer, we will go through and document the operation of the entire system. Once we have proof that the programming is doing what is expected, we can sign off on the completion.
