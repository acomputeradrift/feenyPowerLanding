Questions dealers ask before booking Remote Programming services

Plain answers for custom integrators and dealers evaluating remote programming, commissioning, or troubleshooting support.

Can you help me design an RTI system or specify equipment?

Absolutely. With experience using all of the newest RTI gear (and I have seen it deployed), I know the capabilities of each component, what the most common “gotchas” are and where some controllers shine, while others might not be suited for your client. First call is always free while we come up with a plan.

How much will it cost for my project?

Each project is unique but I have spent dozens of hours creating and continually updating an automated RTI specific form / budget calculator. It understands what takes more time to produce and takes into account systems that are built in a scalable way to save time, while allowing us to assign more hours to truly custom programming. My rate is $100/hr USD for design, programming and commissioning.

How long will it take?

My calculator is built specifically to answer this question, it will produce a time budget calculated using the historical data from dozens of completed RTI projects. Once we have that time budget, we can get an idea of how many business days this will take. For most clients, I am able to get the programming to a “site testing / commissioning” state before the project equipment has even arrived on site. Once the equipment is installed, we move immediately into testing. Generally, 20% of the time budget is spent in testing and commissioning after the equipment is installed.

What is your experience in this industry?

For almost 10 years, I built an Electrical Contracting company in Vancouver, BC Canada primarily working in custom homes, eventually specializing in providing lighting control, motorized shading and audio/video integration on top of our electrical services. As part of this journey, I had learn to coordinate with all of the technology in the house and almost always took over the network so I could ensure it all worked together.

In 2017, recognizing a gap in the home control industry for someone with my level of understanding of how modern houses operate, I decided to close down the contracting side and concentrate my energy on home technology control and building user centric interfaces. During COVID, this business model responded well to off site work and the rest is history!

What is the process?

The initial call is to determine scope, timeline and make sure that we are a good fit for success. I will ask a series of questions to determine the current criteria (rooms, sources, equipment, client wants) and to determine the level of UI that is best for your project. Some systems are what I call Overview only (the ability to control all rooms from a single page), some systems are Room based only (navigate to a room, pick your activity) and some systems are both for the most flexibility for multiple user types. For larger systems, we can also create a floor plan based system using the architectural drawing from the build.

From that I will produce a time budget that reflects our current understanding of the scope, usually within 5 minutes of getting all the answers.

If given the go ahead, initial programming will begin immediately with the goal of being ready for testing on the date that the equipment arrives. That way, I am good to go well ahead of whenever your team is onsite for commissioning.
