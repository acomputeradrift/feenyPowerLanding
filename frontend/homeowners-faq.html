Questions homeowners ask before a design and scope

Plain answers for a house in Greater Vancouver, Kelowna, or Los Gatos. This is separate from the dealer FAQ about remote programming for integrators.

What does Feeny Power and Control do for a homeowner?

You contact us about a Lutron, RTI, or Vantage system. Jamie looks at what you need. If consulting is the right next step, he prepares a design and a scope document. You can take that scope to his preferred onsite contractors, or to other companies, and ask them for proposals. The company you hire supplies the equipment and installs it. Jamie then programs the system so the installed work follows the design you agreed. We do not supply the equipment, and we do not install it.

How does a project start?

Start with the project form on the page for the area where the house is. The form asks for the property location, whether this is a new build, a renovation, or an existing system, what you want or what is not working, and how to reach you. Sending the form does not hire us and does not book installation. We review the enquiry and get back to you to discuss whether consulting is the right next step. Jamie uses that conversation to understand the needs. This page does not say how soon the reply will come.

What is the design and scope document for?

It is the written design and the scope of work you and Jamie agree during the paid consulting. You can give that scope to contractors when you ask them to propose. The items in the agreed scope are the ones the document covers. Anything not written there is not included just because this FAQ mentions the service. The exact deliverables are settled in that scope, not fixed as a package on this page.

Can I use Jamie's preferred contractors, or someone else?

Both. Jamie can suggest contractors he prefers for the onsite work. You can also take the same scope to other companies. You are not required to hire a preferred contractor. This site does not publish those companies' names.

Why does one scope make proposals easier to compare?

Contractors are reading the same description of the job, so their proposals are easier to set side by side. That does not guarantee identical bids or prices. Each contractor should say what they would substitute and what they would leave out. Those notes are how you see the differences.

Who supplies the equipment and installs it?

The contractor you hire for the onsite work. Feeny Power and Control does not supply the products and does not do the installation. We also do not keep an office or a showroom in Greater Vancouver, Kelowna, or Los Gatos.

What does the programming cover?

After the equipment is in, Jamie programs it to carry out the design and scope you agreed. That is the step that connects the original plan to the system you live with. Programming is its own agreement, separate from the consulting. Where the programming work is done, and which hours it includes, are part of that agreement. This page does not set them in advance.

Do you work on new builds, renovations, and existing systems?

Yes. A new build or renovation usually needs the design before the onsite work is finished. An existing system starts from what is already installed and what is not working. The form asks which one it is because the questions are different. There is no standard package on this page.

What is paid, and what is agreed later?

Consulting, design, and specification are paid services. The consulting engagement, including what the scope document will contain, is agreed after we talk. It is not agreed by sending the form. Programming is agreed separately, including the hours. This page has no prices, no fixed bid, and no statement that a consulting fee is credited toward programming or toward the contractor's work.

What if the plan or the equipment changes?

Tell Jamie before programming if the rooms change, or if a contractor proposes different equipment or leaves something out. He reviews that change against the design so the programmed system can still match what you agreed. A change that is not reviewed can break that line from the scope to the finished system.

What am I allowed to give a contractor?

You may use the scope document to ask contractors for proposals. This page does not give permission to pass on other project files. Anything beyond the scope needs Jamie's permission.

Which systems is this for?

Lutron, RTI, and Vantage. Those are the platforms this homeowner service is written around. The logos name the products. This site does not state a certification, and it does not promise that every product in those lines will suit every house.
