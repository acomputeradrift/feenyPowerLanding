Questions dealers ask

What does it cost?

I bill hourly, against a time budget you approve before I start. Jobs over $1,000 take a 50% deposit; smaller jobs don’t need one. For RTI jobs, my budget tool gives you real numbers on your project in minutes.

How long will programming take?

It depends on the size of the job, and the time budget tells you up front. I usually get the programming 70–80% done before the equipment arrives. About 20–30% of the budget goes to testing and commissioning once it’s installed.

How do you connect to the site?

Through a site computer or your tech’s laptop, with Chrome Remote Desktop, AnyDesk or a VPN. Your team handles anything physical, and I do the programming and testing remotely.

Can you take over a system another dealer programmed?

Usually, yes. With the file, I can clean it up and carry on. Without it, depending on the system, I can recover the existing programming or rebuild it.

Can you help design the system or spec the equipment?

Yes. I know where each controller shines, and the common gotchas. We can plan it together before you quote the client.

Do you only do RTI?

No. RTI, Lutron, Vantage and Clipsal C-Bus — one programmer for all four.
