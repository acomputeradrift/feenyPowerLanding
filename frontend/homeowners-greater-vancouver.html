What gets in the way on a Greater Vancouver home

Separate trades one house

On a custom home in Greater Vancouver, lighting, shading, and audio are often chosen by different people. We write one design and specification so the keypads, the control system, and the way the rooms are used belong to the same plan.

Decide it before the walls close

New builds and renovations need the control design before cable and keypads are in. We do that consulting, design, and specification. A local AV company supplies the equipment and installs it.

Already installed and hard to live with

If a Lutron, RTI, or Vantage system is already in the house and it is hard to live with, say what is not working. We will talk about whether a design engagement is the right next step. This form is not an installation quote.

Consulting only no installation crew

Jamie Feeny’s own account on this site’s dealer FAQ: for almost 10 years he ran an electrical contracting company in Vancouver, BC, in custom homes, including lighting control, motorized shading, and audio/video. In 2017 he closed that contracting work. We consult, design, and specify. We do not install the system, and we do not keep a showroom in Greater Vancouver.
